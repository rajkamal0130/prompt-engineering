## 3. Dairy-free and seafood-free substitutions

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Avoid                               Possible alternative, if tolerated
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  Milk                                Unsweetened, calcium-fortified soy
                                      milk

  Curd                                Unsweetened dairy-free soy yoghurt

  Paneer                              Plain tofu

  Butter and ghee                     A small amount of suitable
                                      vegetable oil

  Cheese                              Tofu-based preparations

  Cream-based gravies                 Tomato-based or coconut-based
                                      gravies made without dairy

  Whey protein                        A suitable dairy-free plant
                                      protein, if needed
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

**Milk allergy is not the same as lactose intolerance.** Avoid milk
proteins, including casein, whey and milk powder. Check ingredient
labels for milk-derived ingredients and take precautions against
cross-contact. If your seafood allergy is severe, also check for seafood
cross-contact in restaurants, shared cooking equipment and packaged
foods. Do not try a substitute unless you know it is safe for your
allergies.

## 5. Nutrients to pay attention to

A dairy-free vegetarian diet needs some planning to cover the following
nutrients:

-   **Calcium:** Calcium-set tofu, ragi, sesame seeds and
    calcium-fortified plant milk, if tolerated.
-   **Vitamin B12:** Fortified foods or a supplement may be needed;
    discuss this with a healthcare professional.
-   **Iron:** Lentils, beans and leafy greens. Pair plant sources with
    vitamin C-rich foods such as guava, lemon or orange.
-   **Omega-3 fats:** Ground flaxseed, chia seeds and walnuts, if
    tolerated.
-   **Protein:** Dal, legumes, tofu, soya chunks and fortified soy milk.

## 8. Important health note

Because you have milk and seafood allergies, check with an allergist or
registered dietitian about safe foods, label reading, cross-contact
precautions, calcium and vitamin B12 intake. If you have ever had a
severe allergic reaction, do not use foods with uncertain ingredients or
preparation conditions.

This plan is a general starting point, not a substitute for individual
medical or dietetic advice. If you have a medical condition, take
regular medication, are pregnant or breastfeeding, or experience unusual
fatigue or dizziness, seek professional advice before following a
calorie deficit.

**Overall goal:** Build consistent habits, eat enough protein and
vegetables, use sensible portions and increase daily movement gradually.
Avoid skipping meals or following crash diets.
